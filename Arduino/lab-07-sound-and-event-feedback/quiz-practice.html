Question 5 · Coding

Write a standalone two-button sound selector: D2 and D4 use INPUT_PULLUP, and the passive piezo on D8 has a 220 ohm series resistor to its + terminal and its - terminal at GND. With only D2 pressed, sound 700 Hz; with only D4 pressed, sound 1100 Hz; with both or neither pressed, stay silent. Start silent. Follow current levels without delays; no press counting or debounce is required. Issue tone/noTone only when the requested sound changes.

Question 6 · Coding

Build a desk-call limiter with a D2 pull-up button and the D8 piezo circuit shown. Accept a press after 30 ms at a stable LOW. Each accepted request plays 1000 Hz for 120 ms and starts a 2000 ms lockout measured from that acceptance. Ignore events during lockout without extending it. A new event exactly at or after the boundary may be accepted; holding the button must never retrigger. Start ready and silent; use millis and no delay.

Responses are not saved automatically; print to keep a copy.

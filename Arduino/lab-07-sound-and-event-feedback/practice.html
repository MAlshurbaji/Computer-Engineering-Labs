Independent cancellation and priority

Activity 5 · Build it yourself

A cancellable three-chirp cue

Use D2 request and D4 cancel buttons to GND with INPUT_PULLUP, plus D8 through 220 ohm to a passive piezo. Start silent. A 30 ms stable request while idle starts three 900 Hz chirps, 100 ms each with 100 ms gaps. Ignore busy requests. D4 LOW cancels on the next pass and wins simultaneous inputs. Releasing cancel alone must not restart; require a new accepted request. Use millis and no delay.

Independent level and schedule integration

Activity 6 · Build it yourself

A hold-enabled adjustable reminder

Use a D2 pull-up button to GND, D8 piezo through 220 ohm, and a 10k pot across 5V/GND with wiper A0. Start silent. The first observed LOW starts a 100 ms note immediately; while held, start another no sooner than 1000 ms after each actual start. Sample the pot only at note starts, mapping 0..1023 to 500..900 Hz. Releasing stops sound and disables reminders; pressing again starts fresh. No debounce is required for this level-based task. Use millis and no delay.

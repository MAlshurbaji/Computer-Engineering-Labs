A three-place display pointer

Write a complete sketch for the externally powered D9 positional servo with common ground and separate positive rail. Use one array [40, 90, 140] and an index starting at 0. Command 40 at startup, then 90, 140, 40 repeatedly with at least 2000 ms between commands. Advance one entry per elapsed interval even after a late pass; no delay. Activity 6 · Build it yourself

Move one bounded step per deliberate request

Use the external D9 servo supply with common ground, D2 pull-up button to GND, and A0 pot across Uno 5V/GND. Start at 90. Each 30 ms stable press samples A0 once, maps 0..1023 to 30..150 degrees, and moves the stored command at most 10 degrees toward that sampled target with a final clamp. No movement occurs between press events. Holding or releasing does not repeat; every new press samples the knob again. Use no delay.

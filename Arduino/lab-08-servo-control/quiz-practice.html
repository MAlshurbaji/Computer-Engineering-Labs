Question 5 · Coding

Write a standalone pointer controller using a positional servo on D9 and two INPUT_PULLUP buttons, D2 and D4 to GND. Start with a 90 degree command. Only D2 held selects 40 degrees; only D4 held selects 140; both or neither select 90. Every 25 ms move the current command by at most 3 degrees toward the current target, clamping the final step. Use no delay. Include the external regulated 5V servo supply and common-ground wiring in your design; keep its positive rail separate from Uno 5V.

Question 6 · Coding

Write an alternating-trip controller with the externally powered D9 positional servo and a D2 pull-up button to GND. Start idle at 30 degrees. A stable 30 ms press starts travel to 150 in 5-degree steps, one every 40 ms; the first step occurs after 40 ms. Ignore press events while moving. Remain at the destination until a new press starts the reverse trip. Holding the button must not start another trip. Use millis without delay and keep the servo positive supply separate from Uno 5V with common ground.

Responses are not saved automatically; print to keep a copy.

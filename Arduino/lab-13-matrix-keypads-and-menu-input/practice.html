Dependent Selections

Activity 6 · Build it yourself

Choose a supply-zone location

Keep the full lesson scanner and release/debounce rules. Start with no zone or shelf. A/B chooses a zone, clears any shelf and prints ZONE SELECTED. After a zone exists, 1–3 chooses a shelf and prints SHELF SELECTED. # prints LOCATION followed by a space and zone/shelf together only if both exist, then clears both; otherwise print INCOMPLETE. * clears both and prints CLEARED. Ignore all other events.

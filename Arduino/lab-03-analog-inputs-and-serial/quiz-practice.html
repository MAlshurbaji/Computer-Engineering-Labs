Question 5 · Coding

Write a complete sketch for a ribbon-use dial on A0, wired as shown. Report a remaining length starting at 240 cm for raw 0 and ending at 0 cm for raw 1023. Define used centimetres as the integer part of raw × 240 / 1023, then subtract that from 240. Use arithmetic wide enough for the intermediate product on the Uno R3. Start Serial at 9600 baud; read A0 once and print remaining_cm= followed by the whole-centimetre result on one line, then pause 200 ms each loop. This is a setting model, not a physical length measurement.

Question 6 · Coding

Write a complete sketch for an acceptable-setting indicator. The 10 kΩ pot has its outer terminals at 5V and GND and its wiper at A0; the D8 LED has the series resistor shown. Start D8 off and Serial at 9600 baud. Read A0 once per loop; D8 must be on exactly for raw codes 400–620 inclusive. Send raw:number and inside:number on the same line, separated by a tab, where inside is 1 for that interval and 0 otherwise. End the line, then pause 100 ms. Use a numeric Plotter-compatible report and handle both boundary values.

Responses are not saved automatically; print to keep a copy.

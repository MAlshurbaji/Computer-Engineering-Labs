Averaged Hysteresis

Activity 5 · Build it yourself

A steady warm-room reminder

Use TMP36 and amber D6 with 330 ohm. Sample every 500 ms with the usual conversion and inclusive validity. After four consecutive valid samples print mean_C: on at mean >=27 C, off at <=25 C, retain state between. Start off. Any invalid sample prints CHECK, discards the partial group and clears state/output. No delay.

Consecutive Evidence

Activity 6 · Build it yourself

Light after three in-band samples

Use TMP36 and green D5 with 330 ohm. Start off; sample every 300 ms. Three consecutive valid 21–25 C inclusive samples turn on D5; more keep it on. A valid outside sample immediately clears run/output. Invalid data also clears them and prints CHECK. Valid samples print stable_band=0 or 1 at 9600 baud. Saturate the run at three; no delay.

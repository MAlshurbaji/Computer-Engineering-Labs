Create an interruptible completion cue

Write a complete sketch using the full timer circuit and teaching task 2 snapshot and controls. Replace the single DONE sound with two 880 Hz chirps: 100 ms sound, 100 ms silence, 100 ms sound. Begin once on DONE entry and remain red afterward. A DONE press acknowledges to silent IDLE and abandons every remaining cue phase. RUNNING press cancels, including at the deadline.

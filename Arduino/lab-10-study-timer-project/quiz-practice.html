Question 5 · Coding

Build a repeatable study timer using the shown circuit and states IDLE/RUNNING/DONE. Start IDLE and silent. A stable 30 ms press in IDLE or DONE starts RUNNING and samples A0: 0–340 selects 5 s, 341–681 selects 10 s, 682–1023 selects 15 s. Ignore presses during RUNNING, even at the expiry boundary. On expiry enter DONE, show red and request one 880 Hz, 250 ms tone. Green is on only in RUNNING; red only in DONE. A held button never restarts. Use millis without delay; keep the sampled duration fixed during a run.

Question 6 · Coding

Use the shown timer circuit. A stable 30 ms press in IDLE starts RUNNING and samples A0 using integer map from 0–1023 to 6–18 whole seconds. Green indicates RUNNING. Play one 660 Hz, 120 ms reminder when at least half the stored interval has elapsed. At expiry enter DONE, light red and play one 1000 Hz, 300 ms sound. A press in RUNNING cancels to silent IDLE; a press in DONE acknowledges to IDLE. Process presses first, then expiry, then the halfway action, so cancellation or completion suppresses a late halfway reminder. Start with both LEDs off; held buttons create no repeated events. Use millis without delay.

Responses are not saved automatically; print to keep a copy.

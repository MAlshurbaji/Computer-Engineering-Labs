Session Timestamps

Activity 5 · Build it yourself

Measure a reading session across midnight

Add a D2 INPUT_PULLUP button to GND and adopt its startup level without an event. After a 30 ms stable LOW transition, the first press prints START plus the snapshot and stores full seconds; the next prints END plus the snapshot and SECONDS plus elapsed whole seconds, then closes the session. Require release before another press. Use the same startup clock checks, no automatic adjustment or delay, and valid forward-moving local time. Reset abandons an open session.

Independent Daily Histories

Activity 6 · Build it yourself

Keep two desk reminders independent

Use the specified RTC, startup checks and 250 ms polling. At any observed second of 10:05 print Clear the desk.; at any observed second of 10:07 print Refill the pencil tray. Each reminder may print once per complete date during a run, independently of the other. Suppress repeated polls, do not replay missed minutes, and allow a new date to qualify again. Reset clears both histories; no automatic clock setting.

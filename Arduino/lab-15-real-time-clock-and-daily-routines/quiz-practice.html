Question 5 · Coding

Write a standalone overnight desk indicator with the specified DS3231 wiring and a D5 LED through 330 ohm to its anode, cathode to GND. Start the LED off. On successful begin and a clear lostPower flag, sample every 250 ms: turn it on from 22:30 inclusive through midnight until 06:15 exclusive, every day; otherwise off. At 9600 baud print ON or OFF only when the output changes from its startup/off state. On either startup check failure, keep it off and print an error. Do not adjust the RTC automatically or use delay; assume valid forward-moving local time.

Question 6 · Coding

Write a standalone date-safe minute logger using the shown DS3231 circuit and Serial at 9600 baud. Refuse logging if begin fails or lostPower is set at startup; never adjust the clock automatically. Poll every 5000 ms with millis, starting at least 5000 ms after startup. Print the first observed timestamp and then one timestamp whenever a newly observed calendar minute differs from the last printed minute, including hour, day, month and year changes. Repeated samples in that same minute print nothing. If several minutes were missed, print only the current observation; do not invent skipped records. Assume valid forward-moving time and no reset during the run.

Responses are not saved automatically; print to keep a copy.

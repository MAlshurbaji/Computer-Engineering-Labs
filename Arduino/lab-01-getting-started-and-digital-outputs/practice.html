Startup and Repetition

Activity 5 · Build it yourself

Identify a board after reset

Reuse the D8 and D9 LED branches. Start both off. In setup, light both for 900 ms, then make both dark for 400 ms. Afterwards repeat D8 alone for 300 ms, D9 alone for 600 ms, then both dark for 700 ms. The shared startup signal returns only after reset or startup. Write the complete sketch.

Distinct Signal Lengths

Activity 6 · Build it yourself

Distinguish two shelf locations

Write a complete sketch for separately resisted LEDs D8 and D9, both initially off. Repeat a long D8 indication of 900 ms, 300 ms with both dark, a short D9 indication of 300 ms, and 1500 ms with both dark. No both-on stage is allowed; use delay and repeat the whole plan.
